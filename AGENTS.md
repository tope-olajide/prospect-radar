## Commit messages

Commit normally. Write a concise subject plus a body explaining the *why*, and
stop there.

**Do not add a `Generated with …` line, a `Co-Authored-By:` trailer, or any other
attribution footer.** The repository owner has asked for this explicitly; tool
attribution does not belong in their history.
